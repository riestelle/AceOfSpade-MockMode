Privacy & Consent

MockMode — Data Transparency

database What MockMode Stores

Resume Text

Stored temporarily in localStorage for the duration of your session. Cleared when you start a new game.

Interview Answers & Scores

Used only to generate your final verdict. Not stored long-term. No account or login required.

AI API Calls

Your resume and answers are sent to the AI backend (Groq / Gemini) for analysis. Review their privacy policies at groq.com and cloud.google.com.

tune Optional Features

videocam Webcam Confidence Tracking

Uses your webcam and Face API.js to show a confidence meter during the interview. All processing is local — no video is sent externally.

volume_up Voice Output (TTS)

The interviewer speaks questions aloud using your browser's built-in Web Speech API. No external service used.

mic Voice Input (STT)

Answer questions by speaking. Uses the browser's built-in speech recognition. Chrome is recommended for best results.

block What We Don't Do

  • check_circle No account creation or login required
  • check_circle No ads, no tracking pixels, no analytics
  • check_circle No video or audio recorded to any server
  • check_circle Resume text is never shared with third parties beyond the AI provider
