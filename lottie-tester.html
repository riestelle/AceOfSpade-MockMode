▶ HOW TO TRANSFER TO interview.html
1. Drop <script src="https://cdnjs.cloudflare.com/ajax/libs/bodymovin/5.12.2/lottie.min.js"></script> in your <head>.
2. Replace the <svg class="char"> block with a <div id="lottie-interviewer" style="width:160px;height:120px"></div>.
3. Copy the CharacterController class from this file into your interview.js or inline script.
4. Call controller.startTalking() inside your speakText() onstart, and controller.stopTalking() inside onend.
5. Blink and idle run automatically — toggle via controller.setBlinkEnabled(bool).
